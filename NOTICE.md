The background was resized for the game.
The second miss sound was converted to 16-bit PCM at 44100 Hz. Its source
was identified from the recording's metadata and the creator's published
[catalogue](https://oree.storijapan.net/media/sound-of-work/doc/3_sow-blacksmith-ed1.csv).

The three instrumentals were converted to WAV PCM 16-bit at 44100 Hz.
Original character vocals, created and edited by the project team, were added
as separate tracks. Vocal authorship does not transfer authorship of the
instrumentals to the team.
